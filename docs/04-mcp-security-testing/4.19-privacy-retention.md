# 4.19 Privacy, Data Minimization, and Retention (`PRVC`)

[← Chapter 4 overview](../04-mcp-security-testing.md) · [About `docs/tests/`](about-docs-tests.md) · Procedures in [`docs/tests/PRVC/`](../tests/PRVC/)

## Overview

Privacy tests verify **PII handling**, log retention, downstream copying of transcripts, and tenant/customer boundary labeling in context passed to models.

## Why this category matters

MCP blurs lines between chat, tool output, and resource content—each may contain PII subject to regulation and customer contracts.

## Scope

PII in resources/outputs, retention policies, cross-tenant labels, minimization before model calls.

## When to prioritize

Mandatory for **multi-tenant SaaS** and regulated data classes (health, finance, HR).

## Related categories

[CTX](4.08-context-memory.md), [OUTV](4.11-output-handling.md), [GOV](4.14-enterprise-governance.md), [LOG](4.12-logging-detection.md)

## Common test chains

PRVC-01 + RSRC-01; PRVC-03 + CTX-04 + AUTHZ-03.
